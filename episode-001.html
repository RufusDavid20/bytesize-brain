Episode 001 · Cyber scam alert

The ₹25 Delivery Scam

A tiny payment request can be the bait. Read the comic, learn the red flags, and verify delivery updates through the courier’s official app or website.

BYTE + BYTES · YOUR DIGITAL SIDEKICKS
Remember these five rules

Pause. Verify. Click later.

SAFETY CHECK 01

Don’t tap suspicious links

Even if the requested amount is small, an unexpected message link can lead to a fake payment page.

SAFETY CHECK 02

Use official channels

Open the courier’s official app or type its website address yourself to check the shipment.

SAFETY CHECK 03

Protect payment secrets

Never share your UPI PIN, one-time password, password, or card details through an unexpected link.

SAFETY CHECK 04

Spot urgency tricks

“Pay now” and “parcel on hold” are pressure tactics. Stop and check before acting.

SAFETY CHECK 05

When in doubt, stop

If a message feels wrong, contact the courier using contact details from its official site.

Bit & Bytes’ golden rule

“Think before you click. One smart pause can prevent a big problem.”
