First newsletter · Issue 001

The ₹25
Delivery Scam

Your parcel is “stuck.” A text says you only need to pay ₹25 to get it delivered. It feels tiny, routine, and urgent. That’s exactly why it can work.

Scammers may use a small payment request to lure you onto a fake website and steal your card, UPI, or personal details.

Learn the 3 safety checks ↓
MESSAGE · UNKNOWN SENDER
Your parcel is on hold!
Pay ₹25 now to reschedule delivery.
delivery-track-pay.example
SUSPICIOUS!
SAFETY CHECK 01

Don’t tap the message link

Unexpected delivery texts can be fake—even when they use a familiar courier name or logo.

SAFETY CHECK 02

Track it the official way

Open the courier’s official app or type its website address yourself. Check your order there.

SAFETY CHECK 03

Never share payment secrets

Don’t enter card details, UPI PINs, passwords, or one-time codes on a link from an unexpected text.

Already clicked? Stop entering information. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if you shared payment details, and report suspected cybercrime through your country’s official channels.
